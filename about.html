About Us

Making Chipotle Nutrition Easier to Understand

Chipotle Nutrition Calculator is an independent online tool designed to help you easily explore the nutrition information of different Chipotle meal combinations.

Our Purpose

Building a meal at Chipotle can involve many different ingredients and combinations. Our calculator makes it easier to see how your ingredient choices affect the nutrition of your meal.

Simply select the ingredients you want and instantly see estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar, and sodium.
